# Full scoring audit — November 15, 2026

Reviewed all 57 configurations across Cost, Eco, Trips, Comfort, Assistance and optional Upkeep: 342 category results. This is a source/input consistency and calculation audit with targeted fresh source checks. It is not 57 new road tests, dealer quotes or independent lifecycle studies. The before values below are the local comparison after the trip correction and before this general audit.

## Findings and corrections

- **Cost:** fixed the $121 EV Road Improvement Fee incorrectly charged at initial new-dealer registration; it remains on four renewals. Each EV’s OTD and five-year cost fall $121. Added pickup weight-fee planning allowances of $24/year for Maverick and $80/year for Santa Cruz, raising five-year costs $120/$400. Published curb weight guides the allowance; configured registered unladen weight and other local fees require a quote. Sources: [DMV registration](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-registration/registration-fees/), [DMV weight fees](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/vehicle-industry-registration-procedures-manual-2/appendix-1f-fees/), [Ford specifications](https://wwwdevac.fromtheroad.ford.com/content/dam/fordmediasite/us/en/library/2026/specs/2026_Ford_Maverick_Specs.pdf), [Hyundai XRT](https://www.hyundaiusa.com/us/en/vehicles/2026-santa-cruz/xrt).
- **Eco:** removed unsupported MPG/battery/redesign point adjustments. Single historical references retain their score; multiple references use their unadjusted arithmetic mean, a disclosed proxy convention rather than a new lifecycle model. Equinox EV now uses its same-model FWD reference of 52 instead of an unrelated IONIQ proxy scored 60. Reference selection still matters and does not become scientifically validated by averaging. [Published historical table](https://greenercars.org/greenercars-ratings/).
- **Comfort:** removed 12 unsupported ride/noise departures from the shared 3/5 baseline. Electric propulsion, luxury branding, low price, wheels and sport positioning do not establish comparable ride/noise measurements. Kept selected-seat and control-access estimates, explicitly editorial and preference-dependent, and exposed all four component rationales. Solterra Premium receives its conventional powered-gate credit; Limited kick activation remains excluded. [Subaru trim matrix](https://www.subaru.com/vehicles/solterra/2026/specs-trim.html).
- **Assistance:** Solterra changes from [4,4,3] to [4,5,4] for documented steering-assisted Lane Change Assist and camera-based Driver Monitor. This matches the existing capability rubric. Other recorded assistance profiles remain unchanged; a feature’s presence is not a measured performance or safety advantage. Toyota bZ’s Limited-grade lane-change/TJA features are not borrowed by XLE. [Subaru features](https://www.subaru.com/vehicles/solterra/2026/features.html), [Toyota bZ equipment](https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-bz-all-electric-suv-adds-range-charging-and-exterior-updates-for-2026/).
- **Upkeep:** retained shared disruption/service-access baselines and the DOE-based EV routine-service distinction. No supported model-specific reliability or local repair-access difference was established. Cost includes dollar allowances; optional Upkeep concerns time/effort. Its default remains zero to avoid unintentionally counting maintenance twice.
- **Trips:** retained the preceding full-cohort correction. Access, carrying and replenishment remain separate. A shared EV baseline does not establish equal real-world road-trip performance; actual route testing can justify a difference. No new route or gear-fit measurement was performed.

## Verification and limits

All 67 stored historical reference uses (47 distinct records) were matched against the published table on make, model, score, engine, drivetrain, certification, city/highway fields and weight class. The 20 retained historical assessments were preserved. All 37 proxies remain estimates. Current-model energy records retain their year, make, model-family and powertrain validation; this pass did not independently re-download all 55 EPA records or re-quote all prices.

Reviewed cost signs and units, destination/options/tax once each, PHEV gasoline/electric mileage split, EPA AC consumption without adding charging losses twice, five registrations, subscriptions for every credited paid feature, OTD budget, component bounds, ties, cohort bands, totals and shared rankings/Pareto payloads. Registration renewal values remain planning factors, not DMV depreciation tables. Energy prices, resale, insurance, repair reserves and recurring fee amounts are held at the disclosed scenario; future inflation is not forecast.

Validation now also rejects unsupported ride/noise changes, missing Comfort rationales, arbitrary Eco offsets and pickup registration without an explicit weight-fee allowance. Written evidence fields are review requirements, not automated proof of research quality. A +2 remains a relative cohort band, not an absolute quality or confidence rating. Lower uncertainty does not add points.
