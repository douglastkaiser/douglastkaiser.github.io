How scoring works

Scoring method

×

Equal evidence earns equal scores

Each assessed category orders its underlying values, then places each group of equal values by its midpoint percentile: (number strictly better + half the tied group) / assessed count. Below one-third earns +1; above two-thirds earns −1; boundaries and everything between earn 0. Groups stay together, so tier sizes vary. Category ranks are shared. Adding a candidate can change relative tiers; these are shortlist comparisons, not absolute quality ratings.

Total = sum of each category score × its weight. Five categories start at 1×; Maintenance starts at 0×. Tiers remain fixed while sliders change. Equal totals sort by lower five-year cost, then name. All weights at zero sort by cost. Reset restores defaults.

A dash means unscored. Missing evidence contributes zero, is excluded from that category’s percentile calculation and does not increase the other weights. It is not a middle-tier result. Eco coverage is 20/45 vehicles; missing evidence can affect the apparent leaders. Inspect the details before treating small score differences as meaningful.

Ownership: one visible five-year calculation

New vehicles of any body style, estimated at or below $60,000 out the door in Orange County. Prices use the named model year and configuration, published MSRP, destination and selected packages. No assumed dealer discount, markup or contingent rebate. MSRP in the breakdown excludes destination so it is added exactly once. A published US configuration is not a claim of current stock at a particular dealer. Model years may overlap during the sales year.

Five-year cost = OTD − resale + gasoline + electricity + four registration renewals + insurance + routine upkeep + repair reserve + selected assistance subscriptions. Financing is excluded. First registration and sales tax appear once, inside OTD. Roof carriers and winter tires are extra for every vehicle; no carrier purchase is assumed in the base cost.

Common scenario: 12,000 miles/year for five years; 7.75% Irvine sales tax baseline (other OC cities differ); $85 taxable document fee; current DMV registration/CHP/TIF/VLF schedules plus a $30 annual county-fee allowance. Renewal value factors are 90%, 80%, 70%, 60% of purchase price, explicit planning approximations rather than a DMV calculation. EV road fees are included. Rates are held constant for this comparison.

Nearby public charging is assumed available at ordinary local electricity cost, $0.344/kWh, with no installation charge, availability penalty or retail charging markup, as requested. This is a comparison assumption, not a posted public-station tariff. Gasoline is held at the checked OC AAA reference: $5.5049 regular / $5.9038 premium per gallon. The EPA record’s fuel grade is costed; premium-fuel references do not establish that regular is prohibited. EPA wall-energy consumption already includes charging losses; none are added twice.

PHEVs use a disclosed 60% electric-mile scenario and 40% gasoline miles; this assumes regular access to charging and is not an EPA prediction for your driving. Gas-mode MPG and electrical consumption are separate. Resale uses the identified historical model or segment depreciation benchmark; new generations/powertrains use segment assumptions, not invented exact-build forecasts. The applied fraction and its scope appear in each Cost assessment.

Insurance is a common $2,500/year allowance, not a personalized or model-specific quote. Routine upkeep allowances are 10¢/mile gas, 9¢ hybrid/PHEV, and 6¢ EV, including tires. Repairs add a common $200/year reserve. These round planning inputs are not measured 2026 expenses; the DOE study supports the maintenance-cost distinction, not these exact allowances. Resale and insurance are substantial uncertainties; compare quotes and sensitivity before a purchase.

Comfort includes the liftgate

40% ride/noise + 30% seating + 20% cabin ergonomics + 10% verified power liftgate. Each component is 0–5; a selected, documented power liftgate earns 5 on that component and a manual or unverified opening earns 0. Automatic trunk unlatching alone is not a powered liftgate. Body style and power liftgate never determine eligibility. Comfort does not include driver assistance.

Ride, noise and seating judgments are editorial estimates based on the named configuration’s equipment and packaging, not instrumented road tests. A luxury badge, screen size or trim price does not independently establish comfort. Notes identify the tradeoffs; small component differences need a test drive.

Eco: comparable historical lifecycle evidence only

ACEEE Green Scores include manufacturing, energy supply, use and disposal impacts. Published US coverage ends at model year 2024. A scored row preserves a named, comparable historical configuration, its original score and the relevant wheel, emissions or model-year caveat. It is not a measured 2026/2027 result. Major redesigns, changed powertrains and unrelated models do not inherit a score.

No direct MPG-versus-MPGe comparison, custom lifecycle formula, battery penalty or numeric mixing with European Green NCAP is used. Missing comparable lifecycle data stays visibly unscored; efficient use-phase consumption alone is not a full environmental assessment.

Mountain trips: three adults and their gear

40% carrying usefulness + 35% winter access + 25% trip practicality. Assess two front occupants, one rear adult, bags and long ski/snowboard gear. Maximum cargo with every rear seat folded does not prove fit. Sedan access and low clearance affect usefulness; AWD alone does not prove good winter tires or stopping ability.

Carrying estimates use the manufacturer’s packaging evidence, with verified dimensions where available. Gear fit is unmeasured. Roof routes remain conditional on exact roof, bars/feet, load rating, carrier capacity and clearance. Roof loads add drag, height and handling/loading costs. Current EV range and charging hardware inform trip estimates, without claiming an exact cold-weather or roof-load range penalty.

Commute assistance: selected equipment, not options you did not buy

40% stop/go adaptive cruise + 35% continuous lane centering + 25% monitoring/usability. Select the lowest published trim/package with both capabilities where verified. A lane-departure warning or corrective lane-keeping system receives no continuous-centering credit. A manual describing optional equipment does not prove trim fitment. Unknown capability earns no credit and is disclosed.

Costs include selected assistance options and any credited paid subscription over five years; temporary trials do not establish permanent entitlement. Unselected hands-free upgrades receive no credit. Honda/Acura lane-centering operating speeds, restart behavior and other system limits remain important. These are capability estimates, not independent performance or safety ratings; IIHS safeguards ratings are not steering-quality scores.

Maintenance is optional and uncertain

50% fewer disruptions + 30% less routine service effort + 20% service access. This is a low-confidence editorial comparison and starts at 0×. Historical powertrain continuity, scheduled work, warranty context and diagnostics inform the notes. New hybrid and EV designs do not inherit measured current-year reliability from a brand or predecessor.

Dollar service and repair allowances are already in Cost. This optional category emphasizes time, effort and disruption, so consider overlap when weighting it. RepairPal describes a historical methodology; the components here are not RepairPal ratings, a local shop census, failure probabilities or numeric repairability scores.

Editorial component rubric

0 = absent or unverified credited capability; 1 = very constrained; 2 = constrained; 3 = workable baseline; 4 = useful capability with caveats; 5 = unusually strong fit. Each assessment shows its inputs and rationale. Unknown is not evidence of absence; uncertain fitment is recorded separately. Capability components describe planning usefulness, not laboratory results or failure rates. No hidden confidence bonus or penalty is applied.

Every removed and newly screened candidate is documented in the audit report. Remaining gaps include dealer quotes, individual insurance, actual gear fit, future resale and current-model lifecycle/reliability measurements.
