How scoring works

Scoring method

×
Forced tiers

Each category contains exactly twelve +1, twelve 0, and twelve −1 ratings. Ties are broken deterministically by vehicle name solely to keep the requested 12/12/12 split; tied cars are not meaningfully different.

Cost

Ranks estimated five-year ownership spending: OTD purchase price minus assumed resale value, plus energy, insurance, upkeep and registration. Excludes financing. These carried-forward estimates are rough planning inputs, not researched vehicle-specific ownership quotes. Lower is better.

Efficiency

EPA-style combined MPG for gasoline vehicles versus MPGe for EVs, where 33.7 kWh is treated as the energy in one gallon of gasoline. This compares energy use, not pump cost. The NX 350h Premium AWD has been corrected to 39 MPG.

Snow, comfort and looks

Editorial relative judgments, not instrumented test results. Snow considers drivetrain, clearance, tires, and winter-trip practicality. Looks is subjective. Winter tires can outweigh a vehicle's drivetrain score.

Eligibility

New vehicles, factory powered rear hatch/liftgate, estimated out-the-door price under $60,000; compact-ish crossovers, hatchbacks and liftbacks. Confirm specific model-year equipment and dealer OTD quote before purchase.
