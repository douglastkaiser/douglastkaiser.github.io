# Car rankings accuracy audit

Research checked November 14, 2026. Baseline: `ea354ee26effa9f352d7a7f1f9f255caf2ca6930` (fetched and fast-forwarded from `main`). This report is a dated snapshot; `data.json` contains the research inputs and `index.html` is generated.

## Result and limits

- Screened **58 model/powertrain candidates**: **45 ranked**, **13 excluded or held out**. Compared with the old 36-row field, 24 configurations were retained/reselected, 12 removed, and 21 added. No cohort-size or brand quota.
- Body styles: 30 crossovers, 9 hatchbacks, 6 sedans. Powertrains: 10 EV, 13 Gas, 18 Hybrid, 4 PHEV. Body style and powered rear opening are not eligibility rules.
- **43 exact model-year EPA energy records**, plus two Toyota manufacturer-published EPA references/conversions where no matching row appeared in the checked CSV. EPA passenger/luggage dimensions are retained where available; exterior measurements and actual gear fit are not invented.
- **20 comparable historical lifecycle anchors**; the other 25 display an unscored dash. All ten selected EV configurations lack a retained comparable lifecycle anchor. This coverage gap matters: zero contribution is uncertainty, not evidence of environmental parity with a hybrid.
- Published pricing and feature matrices support reference configurations. They do **not** establish present stock at a particular California dealer, an orderable build with no unwanted port options, future resale, individual insurance, instrumented comfort, exact board fit or current-model failure rates. Comfort, trips and maintenance remain identified editorial judgments.
- A held-out model is not automatically a bad car or unavailable. See the decision table; where an exact assisted package/price was not established, the report says so rather than manufacturing a score.

## Confirmed problems and corrections

1. **Alphabetical scoring artifacts.** The old generator forced 12/12/12 tiers. Identical evidence crossed boundaries in Eco, Mountain, Commute and Maintenance; Commute had only six distinct composite values for 36 cars. Name affected score, not just display order. The new midpoint rule keeps ties together and works for arbitrary cohort sizes.
2. **Unexplained costs.** All 36 old Cost assessments had an empty source list and no stored breakdown. OTD/five-year numbers are now computed from price, destination, selected fees/options, taxes/registration, resale, energy, insurance/upkeep allowances, repair reserves and selected subscriptions. Derived totals are not manually stored in research data.
3. **Inherited Comfort ordering.** The old 1–36 ordinal had no component rubric and heavily separated premium brands. It has been removed. Comfort now uses ride/noise 40%, seating 30%, ergonomics 20%, verified power liftgate 10%. Component rationale is visible, and assistance is separate.
4. **Generation/cross-model lifecycle substitutions.** The old CX-50/Forester used RAV4 anchors, Rogue used a CR-V anchor and Mach-E used Model Y. New LEAF, bZ/Solterra, RAV4, UX powertrain and other redesigns cannot simply inherit predecessor results. Unmatched configurations are now unscored. Original 2024 records, including powertrain, drive, emissions, city/highway fields and weight class, are saved for retained anchors.
5. **Trim and energy mismatches.** Replaced expensive power-door-driven trims where a cheaper assisted trim was documented. The 2026 CX-5 Premium Plus has CTS and is credited. CX-30/Mazda3 CTS selections require turbo grades with their corresponding EPA consumption. Elantra LFA alone did not prove SCC: the current matrix restricts SCC to Limited, which is the selected trim. Low-range IONIQ 5/EV6/bZ and LEAF wheel variants use their own EPA records.
6. **Unavailable California choice.** Removed the listed Corsair Premiere/Reserve following the manufacturer certification disclosure. MINI was removed as requested. Current regular IONIQ 6 US availability was not assumed from the nameplate; unquoted remaining 2025 stock is held out.
7. **Hidden paid assistance.** BMW X1 includes the documented $500 Driving Assistance Plus option. Both Tesla builds explicitly include five years of $99/month FSD (Supervised), not a trial or presumed free entitlement. Base Chevrolet EVs retain ACC but receive no continuous-centering credit for corrective lane keeping. Equinox’s manual also describes optional Hands-On Steering Assist with connected-plan conditions; it is not proof of selected-trim hardware or entitlement. That capability and complete Super Cruise build prerequisites/renewals remain unselected pending verified fitment and price.

## Reproducible calculation and scoring

- Eligibility: new vehicle, all body styles, estimated OTD <= $60,000 in the Irvine/Orange County scenario. Selected US configurations are checked for known California restrictions; no local VIN-level inventory claim.
- Equal-value midpoint percentile = `(strictly better count + tied count / 2) / assessed count`. Below 1/3 is +1; above 2/3 is −1; exact boundaries and the middle are 0. Shared values receive shared category ranks. Alphabetical ordering only breaks final display ties after weighted total and calculated cost.
- Missing Eco is excluded from that category denominator, displayed as a dash and contributes zero without reweighting other categories. Category weights remain five at 1×, optional Maintenance at 0×, range 0–3× in half steps.
- Five-year cost = `OTD − estimated resale + gasoline + electricity + four registration renewals + insurance + routine upkeep + repair reserve + selected subscriptions`. Sales tax and first registration occur once, in OTD; MSRP excludes destination and each selected fee is itemized.
- 12,000 miles/year × five years; charging assumed close by and at ordinary electricity cost, $0.344/kWh, with no installation or access penalty. This is the user scenario, not a retail public charging tariff. AAA OC checked reference prices are held at $5.5049/gal regular and $5.9038/gal premium. No extra losses are added to EPA AC consumption.
- PHEVs assume 60% electric / 40% gasoline miles. This is an explicit usage assumption, not a measured personal utility factor. Two Toyota references convert published MPGe to planning electrical consumption with `33.7 × 100 / MPGe`; blended engine use is a limitation of that approximation. Direct EPA kWh/100-mile records are used when available.
- Irvine tax baseline 7.75%; taxable document-fee allowance $85. DMV uses current $76 registration + $34 CHP + value-based TIF + .65% VLF + EV RIF $121, plus a disclosed $30 county-fee allowance. Four renewal vehicle-value factors (.9/.8/.7/.6) are approximations; other OC cities and real DMV quotes differ.
- Resale applies the named historical model depreciation where used, otherwise the 2026 study’s segment result (35.4% hybrid depreciation, 57.2% EV, 44.9% SUV or 41.8% overall). These are statistics for older cars, not 2031 exact-trim forecasts. Every Cost assessment identifies its scope.
- Common allowances: insurance $2,500/year; routine upkeep including tires 10¢/mile gas, 9¢ hybrid/PHEV, 6¢ EV; repair reserve $200/year. These are transparent round assumptions, not personalized insurance or measured 2026 model repair costs. DOE provides historical maintenance context only. They intentionally avoid unsupported model-specific cost precision.
- Roof racks/boxes and winter tires are extra for all entries and excluded from the base ownership scenario. Trip assessments flag where these may be needed. Financing and conditional rebates/expired federal purchase credits are excluded.

Common references: [EPA downloadable vehicle data](https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/epadata/vehicles.csv.zip), [ACEEE published Green Scores](https://greenercars.org/greenercars-ratings/), [iSeeCars five-year depreciation study](https://www.iseecars.com/cars-that-hold-their-value-study), [SCE average electricity rate](https://www.sce.com/save-money/rates-financing/sce-rate-advisory), [AAA Orange County fuel prices](https://gasprices.aaa.com/?state=CA), [CDTFA city tax rates](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/rates.aspx), [California DMV registration schedules](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-registration/registration-fees/), [DOE scheduled-maintenance comparison](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/vehicles/articles/fotw-1190-june-14-2021-battery-electric-vehicles-have-lower-scheduled), [IRS clean vehicle credit eligibility](https://www.irs.gov/clean-vehicle-tax-credits).

## Maintenance and validation

Edit `projects/car-rankings/data.json` for research and `template.html` for presentation. Run `python3 scripts/build_car_rankings.py` to regenerate the tracked offline page. Derived costs/scores in research inputs, invalid numeric data, mismatched EPA year/powertrain/family, unknown evidence references, unsupported liftgate/centering credit and over-budget builds are rejected.

- `python3 scripts/test_car_rankings.py`: 30 standard-library checks for tied/boundary/all-equal/missing evidence, arbitrary cohort sizes, order/name independence, costs/units/PHEV split, selected packages/subscriptions, fees, no double counting, generation, the Projects-page count, escaping and input rejection.
- `python3 scripts/build_car_rankings.py --check`: confirms generated HTML matches data/template.
- `python3 scripts/test_car_rankings_browser.py`: Chromium/Playwright, 45 rows × nine weight scenarios (default, all-zero, each category alone, mixed half weights), reset, row/dialog total parity, offline operation with zero HTTP requests, 1280px desktop and 390/320px mobile, keyboard focus/Escape, deep links and no-JavaScript fallback. Screenshots are saved under `/tmp/car-rankings-preview`.
- The Pages workflow runs unit checks and generator consistency before publishing commits pushed to `main`.

Remaining purchase checks: actual dealer build/fees/inventory; personal insurance; resale variation; real three-person/gear fit and carrier/roof restrictions; winter tires/chains; current-model comfort, reliability and lifecycle measurements. Base Chevrolet Super Cruise quotes, uncertain held-out luxury configurations and actual PHEV charging share need additional evidence before they receive credit.
