# Car rankings accuracy audit

Research checked November 14, 2026. Scoring update baseline: `f9ac7bcbf8a5353ffc5173ac2befa81201346f90` (fetched and fast-forwarded from `main`). Original configuration research baseline: `ea354ee26effa9f352d7a7f1f9f255caf2ca6930`. This report includes the five-level/full-assessment update and commute-assistance re-audit; `data.json` contains the research inputs and `index.html` is generated.

## Result and limits

- Screened **58 model/powertrain candidates**: **45 ranked**, **13 excluded or held out**. Compared with the old 36-row field, 24 configurations were retained/reselected, 12 removed, and 21 added. No cohort-size or brand quota.
- Body styles: 30 crossovers, 9 hatchbacks, 6 sedans. Powertrains: 10 EV, 13 Gas, 18 Hybrid, 4 PHEV. Body style and powered rear opening are not eligibility rules.
- **43 exact model-year EPA energy records**, plus two Toyota manufacturer-published EPA references/conversions where no matching row appeared in the checked CSV. EPA passenger/luggage dimensions are retained where available; exterior measurements and actual gear fit are not invented.
- **45 complete Eco assessments**: 20 unchanged comparable historical lifecycle anchors and 25 documented editorial proxies, including all ten EVs. Every car has six numeric category scores and participates in Pareto comparisons. Proxy estimates are explicitly distinguished from published current-model ratings.
- Published pricing and feature matrices support reference configurations. They do **not** establish present stock at a particular California dealer, an orderable build with no unwanted port options, future resale, individual insurance, instrumented comfort, exact board fit or current-model failure rates. Comfort, trips and maintenance remain identified editorial judgments.
- A held-out model is not automatically a bad car or unavailable. See the decision table; where an exact assisted package/price was not established, the report says so rather than manufacturing a score.

## Confirmed problems and corrections

1. **Alphabetical scoring artifacts.** The old generator forced 12/12/12 tiers. Identical evidence crossed boundaries in Eco, Mountain, Commute and Maintenance; Commute had only six distinct composite values for 36 cars. Name affected score, not just display order. The new midpoint rule keeps ties together and works for arbitrary cohort sizes.
2. **Unexplained costs.** All 36 old Cost assessments had an empty source list and no stored breakdown. OTD/five-year numbers are now computed from price, destination, selected fees/options, taxes/registration, resale, energy, insurance/upkeep allowances, repair reserves and selected subscriptions. Derived totals are not manually stored in research data.
3. **Inherited Comfort ordering.** The old 1–36 ordinal had no component rubric and heavily separated premium brands. It has been removed. Comfort now uses ride/noise 40%, seating 30%, ergonomics 20%, assessed power liftgate 10%. Component rationale is visible, and assistance is separate.
4. **Generation/cross-model lifecycle substitutions.** The old CX-50/Forester used RAV4 anchors, Rogue used a CR-V anchor and Mach-E used Model Y. New LEAF, bZ/Solterra, RAV4, UX powertrain and other redesigns cannot simply inherit predecessor results. The five-level update assigns unmatched configurations explicit editorial proxy assessments with named source records and adjustment rationales; original same-model historical records remain separate. Original 2024 records, including powertrain, drive, emissions, city/highway fields and weight class, are saved for retained anchors.
5. **Trim and energy mismatches.** Replaced expensive power-door-driven trims where a cheaper assisted trim was documented. The 2026 CX-5 Premium Plus has CTS and is credited. CX-30/Mazda3 CTS selections require turbo grades with their corresponding EPA consumption. Elantra LFA alone did not prove SCC: the current matrix restricts SCC to Limited, which is the selected trim. Low-range IONIQ 5/EV6/bZ and LEAF wheel variants use their own EPA records.
6. **Unavailable California choice.** Removed the listed Corsair Premiere/Reserve following the manufacturer certification disclosure. MINI was removed as requested. Current regular IONIQ 6 US availability was not assumed from the nameplate; unquoted remaining 2025 stock is held out.
7. **Hidden paid assistance.** BMW X1 includes the documented $500 Driving Assistance Plus option. Both Tesla builds explicitly include five years of $99/month FSD (Supervised), not a trial or presumed free entitlement. Base Chevrolet EVs retain ACC but receive no continuous-centering credit for corrective lane keeping. Equinox’s manual also describes optional Hands-On Steering Assist with connected-plan conditions; it is not proof of selected-trim hardware or entitlement. Best estimate for the selected LT1 is no continuous centering, explicitly labeled as estimated fitment. CX-50 Hybrid Preferred is also estimated to lack CTS based on its available grade description. Neither estimate adds an option or subscription cost.

## Reproducible calculation and scoring

- Eligibility: new vehicle, all body styles, estimated OTD <= $60,000 in the Irvine/Orange County scenario. Selected US configurations are checked for known California restrictions; no local VIN-level inventory claim.
- Equal-value midpoint percentile = `(strictly better count + tied count / 2) / full cohort count`. Five bands at 20/40/60/80% give +2/+1/0/−1/−2; exact boundaries go toward zero. Shared values receive shared category ranks. Alphabetical ordering only breaks final display ties after weighted total and calculated cost.
- All six metrics have finite assessment values. Estimates participate in the full-cohort denominator and all Pareto networks; zero means middle band. Rankings, Pareto and dialogs use one canonical score map. The scale selector and missing-evidence recovery controls have been removed; legacy scale links retain view/dialog behavior and always use five levels. Category weights remain five at 1×, optional Maintenance at 0×, range 0–3× in half steps.
- Five-year cost = `OTD − estimated resale + gasoline + electricity + four registration renewals + insurance + routine upkeep + repair reserve + selected subscriptions`. Sales tax and first registration occur once, in OTD; MSRP excludes destination and each selected fee is itemized.
- 12,000 miles/year × five years; charging assumed close by and at ordinary electricity cost, $0.344/kWh, with no installation or access penalty. This is the user scenario, not a retail public charging tariff. AAA OC checked reference prices are held at $5.5049/gal regular and $5.9038/gal premium. No extra losses are added to EPA AC consumption.
- PHEVs assume 60% electric / 40% gasoline miles. This is an explicit usage assumption, not a measured personal utility factor. Two Toyota references convert published MPGe to planning electrical consumption with `33.7 × 100 / MPGe`; blended engine use is a limitation of that approximation. Direct EPA kWh/100-mile records are used when available.
- Irvine tax baseline 7.75%; taxable document-fee allowance $85. DMV uses current $76 registration + $34 CHP + value-based TIF + .65% VLF + EV RIF $121, plus a disclosed $30 county-fee allowance. Four renewal vehicle-value factors (.9/.8/.7/.6) are approximations; other OC cities and real DMV quotes differ.
- Resale applies the named historical model depreciation where used, otherwise the 2026 study’s segment result (35.4% hybrid depreciation, 57.2% EV, 44.9% SUV or 41.8% overall). These are statistics for older cars, not 2031 exact-trim forecasts. Every Cost assessment identifies its scope.
- Common allowances: insurance $2,500/year; routine upkeep including tires 10¢/mile gas, 9¢ hybrid/PHEV, 6¢ EV; repair reserve $200/year. These are transparent round assumptions, not personalized insurance or measured 2026 model repair costs. DOE provides historical maintenance context only. They intentionally avoid unsupported model-specific cost precision.
- Roof racks/boxes and winter tires are extra for all entries and excluded from the base ownership scenario. Trip assessments flag where these may be needed. Financing and conditional rebates/expired federal purchase credits are excluded.

Common references: [EPA downloadable vehicle data](https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/epadata/vehicles.csv.zip), [ACEEE published Green Scores](https://greenercars.org/greenercars-ratings/), [iSeeCars five-year depreciation study](https://www.iseecars.com/cars-that-hold-their-value-study), [SCE average electricity rate](https://www.sce.com/save-money/rates-financing/sce-rate-advisory), [AAA Orange County fuel prices](https://gasprices.aaa.com/?state=CA), [CDTFA city tax rates](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/rates.aspx), [California DMV registration schedules](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-registration/registration-fees/), [DOE scheduled-maintenance comparison](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/vehicles/articles/fotw-1190-june-14-2021-battery-electric-vehicles-have-lower-scheduled), [IRS clean vehicle credit eligibility](https://www.irs.gov/clean-vehicle-tax-credits).

## Commute-assistance re-audit

The previous inputs assigned 40 of 45 configurations the same `[4, 4, 3]` profile. Under either the main-branch three-level method or the five-level method, that tied majority received 0. The slider correctly multiplied category scores, but changing Assist could only penalize the five lower-scoring cars. Many commute notes were copied from general cabin/gear descriptions rather than explaining assistance.

Retained the 40/35/25 component weights and five-level tied-group percentile rule. Replaced the generic commute text with the selected system, exact package context and three explicit capability/usability rationales for every configuration. The refined component labels are Traffic/speed control, Steering coverage and Supervision/usability. Category scores remain fixed while the weight changes totals and order.

Newly credited differences are based on selected features, with sources and caveats:

- Model 3/Model Y: the already-costed FSD (Supervised) subscription supports route turns, lane changes and intersections, with cabin-camera attention monitoring. [Tesla owner manual](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/en_us/GUID-2CB60804-9CEA-4F4B-8B04-09B991368DC5.html). This is broader task coverage, not autonomous driving or a tested safety advantage.
- CX-5 Premium Plus: selected CTS adds Lane Change Assist and Driver Monitoring, documented by the [2026 packaging announcement](https://news.mazdausa.com/2026-01-13-Mazda-Announces-Pricing-and-Packaging-for-All-New-2026-Mazda-CX-5). These are not borrowed by the older CX-30/Mazda3 CTS assessments.
- IONIQ 5 SE Standard Range: the [2026 manufacturer trim matrix](https://www.hyundaiusa.com/us/en/vehicles/2026-ioniq-5/se-standard-range) lists SCC2, navigation-based curve control and Forward Attention Warning. Higher-grade HDA2/lane-change benefits remain excluded.
- Niro EX and EV6 Light: selected navigation-based speed control receives explicit credit. The [2026 EV6 matrix](https://www.kia.com/us/en/ev6/specs-compare) confirms the model year and Light configuration; current Niro/Seltos generic comparison pages have moved to 2027, so they were not substituted for the retained 2026 fitment references.
- Tiguan S and GTI S: IQ.DRIVE Travel Assist plus Emergency Assist supports a higher editorial supervision/usability estimate. The [VW system guide](https://www.vw.com/en/iq-drive/travel-assist.html) describes hands-on assistance and response to driver inactivity; no camera-based gaze-tracking claim is made.
- Mach-E Select remains on selected adaptive cruise/centering, without unselected BlueCruise. Base Bolt excludes unselected Super Cruise. HR-V/RDX retain lower steering coverage for their speed-limited LKAS. CX-50/Equinox estimated fitment remains explicitly identified.

The revised data has eight distinct raw commute composites rather than three. The eight configurations above receive +2 instead of 0; 32 ordinary profiles remain tied at 0 and five limited profiles at −2. Equal evidence is still kept together, so +1/−1 can be empty. The estimates distinguish supported tasks; they do not invent exact-build performance differences to fill all bands.

Regression checks exercise actual keyboard Home/half-step/End input and mobile pointer input, confirm exact contribution changes in rows and dialogs, verify that rankings move and base score pills remain stable, and check reset. Unit checks pin meaningful selected-feature comparisons and require a system plus three nonempty component rationales.

## Lifecycle proxy assessment register

`eco.assessment_score` is the value ranked across all 45 cars. `basis` is `historical` or `proxy`. Historical values must equal the original published `benchmark_record.score`. Proxies retain empty exact-match benchmark fields and store named `proxy_records` plus `adjustment_note`; their assessment values are editorial judgments, not newly published ACEEE results.

The [ACEEE 2024 Greenest List](https://www.aceee.org/news/electric-vehicles-top-2024-greenercars-rankings-prices-drop/) supplies the published Leaf (66), bZ4X (66), EV6 (63), Ioniq 5 (62), Camry LE Hybrid (63) and RAV4 Prime (64) records used below. Other proxies use the original retained source records in `data.json`. Same-model/model-family history is preferred, then powertrain/size/efficiency peers. Adjustments are deliberately coarse and include judgment about manufacturing/battery and use-phase differences; there is no new lifecycle model or direct MPG/MPGe ranking.
